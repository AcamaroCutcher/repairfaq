    Photos of GTE/Sylvania CO2 Laser

    These are a few small GTE/Sylvania CO2 Lasers. The 941 uses a sealed tube with a ZnSe OC mirror at one end and a ZnSe Brewster window at the other. An HR mirror on a three-screw adjustable mount is external (on the flange visible at the end of the tube).
