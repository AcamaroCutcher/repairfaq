Laser Printers

On-line laser printer resources

  • There is a complete textbook on laser printers: "A Laser Printer Book" by Steven Burrows, on-line at: http://www.poota.com/lpbook/00-toc.html.

    While this doesn't have much electronic repair information, it certainly would seem to cover just about everything else including some discussion of common image quality and mechanical problems.

  • Parts Now! has a set of articles on specific laser printer print engine maintenance, repair, parts interchangeability, etc. These can be found at:

    • Parts Now Service Today Archives.

  • UK Toner Cartridges is a toner printer ink cartridge supplier who offers free advice on all things printer related.
