    Photos of Assorted Helium-Cadmium Lasers

      (The following photo provided courtesy of: Daniel Ames (dlames2@aol.com).)

      • View 01 - Almost complete tube probably for HeCd laser.

      (The following photo provided courtesy of: Thomas Galeski (azotic@aol.com).)

      • View 02 - Omnichrome model 326 air-cooled HeCd laser head

    • Omnichrome Model 100 HeCd Laser Power Supply (Gallery)

      The Omni-100 is a very popular power supply which can drive a variety of Omnichrome HeCd laser heads including those that look similar to the model 326, above.
