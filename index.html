    Unaltered mirror · modern companion

    Three decades of repair wisdom, preserved.

    The Sci.Electronics.Repair FAQ and Sam's Laser FAQ are among the largest free collections of practical electronics knowledge ever written: how to troubleshoot TVs, microwave ovens, power supplies, CD players and lasers, with thousands of hand-drawn ASCII schematics. This site keeps a faithful copy online and adds a companion for exploring it.

    Written by Samuel M. Goldwasser and Filip M. Gieszczykiewicz, with contributions from 1,498 people credited by name in the text. The original lives at repairfaq.org.

    Companion

    Every ASCII schematic, redrawn.

    Sam drew his circuits with nothing but keyboard characters, so they would survive any newsgroup, mail client or screen. The companion reads each one and draws it again as a vector schematic with animated current, and as a 3D circuit board you can orbit. Hover a wire to light up everything connected to it. The original ASCII is always one click away.

    “Basic HeNe Laser Power Supply Organization”, from Sam's Laser FAQ - HeNe Laser Power Supplies, by Samuel M. Goldwasser. Diagram page with the full copyright notice.

    How this mirror honors the authors' wishes

    The site's Copyright and Fair Use page sets out how the FAQs may be reproduced. This mirror follows it:

    Unaltered

    Pages are copied byte for byte. Only fonts and colors are restyled, “to be consistent with other pages on the Web site,” as the terms allow.

    Credited and linked

    Every page starts with an HTML comment naming where it came from, and a visible line linking the original on repairfaq.org.

    Notices kept

    Each document's “Author and Copyright” section stays at the top. Each diagram page in the companion repeats it in full.

    Free, no ads

    Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or sponsored links: non-commercial use only, as the authors require.
