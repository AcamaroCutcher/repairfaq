    Photos of Aerotech Helium-Neon Lasers

    These are photos of various Aerotech HeNe lasers, currently only the Syncrolase 100 and LZR2000 metrology laser.

    • Aerotech Syncrolase 100 Helium-Neon Laser (Gallery)

      This is a single frequency stabilized HeNe laser using a mini-induction heater for thermal control rather than the more common resistance heater.

      • View 01 - Photo of Aerotech Syncrolase 100 with external HeNe laser power supply. There was also a version with the HeNe laser power built into the back of the head cylinder.

      • View 02 - Components of Syncrolase 100 (except for DC wall adapter for control board and HeNe laser power supply).

      • View 03 - Left side view of control board.

      • View 04 - Right side view of control board.

      • View 05 - Closeup of cathode-end of HeNe laser tube.

      • View 06 - Closeup of induction heating coil.

    • Aerotech LZR2000 Helium-Neon Laser (Gallery)

      This is a single frequency stabilized HeNe laser based on the Aerotech Syncrolase 100, but repackaged along with an optical receiver specifically for metrology applications.

      • View 01 - Left front view.

      • View 02 - Front closeup view. The laser beam exits the top hole while the return beam enters the bottom hole. The front ring may be rotated 45 degrees right to block the beam or 45 degrees left to use the small "alignment sperture".

      • View 03 - Right back view.

      • View 04 - Back closeup view. The DIN connector is for the SIN/COS (analog) signal while the DB9 connector is for the SIN/COS line driver (digital signal).

      • View 05 - Left front interior view.

      • View 06 - Left side interior view.

      • View 07 - Right back interior view.

      • View 08 - Right side interior view.

      • View 09 - View of left side of tube assembly.

      • View 10 - View of right side of tube assembly.

      • View 11 - Closeupf view of back of laser tube.

      • View 12 - Closeupf view of optical receiver beam sampler assembly.

      • View 13 - Closeup view of the control PCB.

    There is much more information on these Aerotech lasers in Sam's Laser FAQ, in the chapter: Commercial Stabilized HeNe Lasers.
