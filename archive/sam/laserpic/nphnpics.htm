    Photos of Newport Helium-Neon Lasers

    • Newport NL-1 Stabilized Helium-Neon Laser (Gallery)

      This is a dual polarized mode stabilized HeNe laser from Newport and is derived directly from the Laseangle RB-1 but it no longer looks like someone's science fair project. :) As far as I know, the NL-1 was the only stabilized laser ever sold by Newport that was actually manufactured by them. (Newport currently lists the REO SHL but at most, they put the Newport logo on it, if that.)

      • View 01 - Left front view.

      • View 02 - Front closeup view. It's basically a plate with a hole. There was a cap on a chain that could be popped into the hole to serce as a shutter, but that has somehow disappeared.

      • View 03 - NL-1 laser head - right back view.
      • View 04 - NL-1 laser head - back closeup view.
      • View 05 - NL-1 laser head - left front interior view.
      • View 06 - NL-1 laser head - right back interior view.
      • View 07 - NL-1 laser head - left side interior view. The photodiode pre-amp PCB on the left.

      • View 08 - NL-1 laser head - right side interior view. The cylinderical housing contains a Polarizing Beam-Splitter (PBS) that samples one of the polarized modes (P-polarized) and blocks it from exiting the laser. There is also a ~10 percent 45 degree mirror that samples the other polarized mode (S-polarized). The photodiodes are mounted under the copper block.
