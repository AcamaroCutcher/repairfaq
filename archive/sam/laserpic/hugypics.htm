    Photos of Hughes Freqeuncy Doubler for YAG Laser

    This is a beam modifier module (not a complete laser head) for an external 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser. Shoot a 1064 nm beam in one end and the KDP crystal in the frequency doubler produces a 532 nm (green) beam out the other. As can be inferred from its size, this module can handle the beam from a high power Nd:YAG laser!
