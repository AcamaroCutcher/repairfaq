      The terms, and how this mirror follows them

      From the site's Copyright and Fair Use Considerations (version 1.04):

      • “The entire section ‘About the Author and Copyright’ MUST be included when any sections are excerpted.” Mirrored pages are complete, so their notices are intact. Each diagram page in the companion is an excerpt, and shows its document's notice in full at the top.
      • “Documents or excerpts must NOT be altered in any way that affects their meaning.” The pages are byte-for-byte copies. The diagram views are clearly labeled as new drawings, with the original ASCII beside them.
      • “There must be a header, as an HTML comment, at the top of each page showing where the material originated.” Added to every page.
      • “An active link to the original document… clearly labeled as the source.” Every page has a “Source:” line linking its original URL.
      • The HTML look and feel “should not be altered. Changes to font and color schemes are permitted to the extent required to be consistent with other pages on the Web site.” One stylesheet sets fonts and colors to match this site. It changes nothing else.
      • No ads, no charge, non-commercial. This site carries no ads or sponsored links and charges nothing.

      Sam's Laser FAQ adds that “non-commercial use of this material is permitted as long as the source is acknowledged in an appropriate manner.” If you reuse anything you find here, please follow the same terms and link to https://www.repairfaq.org/.

      About this mirror

      The site was downloaded in November 2026 so that this knowledge survives even if the original goes offline. What is hosted where:

      • Pages, schematics, PDFs and most photos are hosted here.
      • The three large photo galleries (laser equipment, repair photos, brochures; 9,317 photos) are hosted at acamarocutcher.github.io/repairfaq-photos, because GitHub Pages limits each site to 1 GB.
      • 56 large videos, slide shows and programs are not hosted. Their links point to repairfaq.org, and a full copy is kept offline.

      If you are one of the authors and would like anything changed or removed, please contact Acamaro Cutcher; it will be done right away.
