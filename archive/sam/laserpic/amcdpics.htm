    Photos of Amoco Diode Pumped Solid State Lasers

    [Only about 3 people per decade visit the Laser Equipment Gallery ;-), the existing format is archaic, and I am lazy. So going forward, we will use the Windows App "Web Album Generator" for most of the collections.]

    Note: Most links open in a single new tab or window depending on your browser's settings.

    These are photos of Amoco DPSS lasers, presently only the 532-EH-50. While Amoco was/is known as an oil company ;-), at one time in the past they were a manufacturer of high quality lasers for NASA, among others.

    More information on these Amoco DPSS lasers can be found in the chapter Solid State Lasers of Sam's Laser FAQ.

    Amoco 532-EH-50 Diode Pumped Solid State Laser Head and Controller

    Amoco 532-EH-50 System

    The 532-EH-50 is a 50 mW (rated) 532 nm (green) DPSS laser with a TEM00 beam profile. The samples I've tested were also Single Longitudinal Mode (SLM) / single frequency, though it is not known if this is spec'd.

    The Web Album of the laser head is a composite of components from two systems: an intact head and only the laser assembly from another one. That's why there are some suble differences, but most would be unnoticeable. The controller is for these heads but wasn't tuned for either one. Descriptions under each photo provide more information on what it is.
