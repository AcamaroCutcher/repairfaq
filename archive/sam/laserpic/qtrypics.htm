    Quantronix YAG Lasers

    • Quantronix Model 114 Laser Head and Power Supply (Gallery)

      The Quantronix 114 is a Q-switched YAG laser rated 35 watts multimode and 10 watts TEM00. The CDRH safety label reads: Nd YAG CW / 50 ps, 300W Max., Class IV Laser Product. A more complete description of this laser can be found in the chapter: Solid State Lasers of Sam's Laser FAQ.
