HP LaserJet 4 - intermittent error code 50

(From: Brian Mathews (icontech@volcano.net).)

I have seen this problem quite a few times and I check the fuser lamp to find it was manufactured by USHIO. After replacement with a Toshiba lamp (not an endorsement, just what the supplier sends) the problem is gone. I haven't ever had this problem and not found that brand of heat lamp in the fuser......
