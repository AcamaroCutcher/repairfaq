    Photos of Sharplan Carbon Dioxide Lasers

    Like the Coherent Carbon Dioxide Lasers, these are also basically commercial versions of the type of flowing gas CO2 laser that can be built at home by a dedicated amateur.
    • Laser Industries Sharplan Model 733 Carbon Dioxide Laser (Gallery)

      (The following 2 photos provided courtesy of: Larry (buckaroo@freenet.edmonton.ab.ca).)

      This is a Sharplan model 733 CO2 Laser used for medical/surgical applications. (Unit was removed from a hospital when it downsized.)

      • View 01 - Overall view. This is the main laser unit containing the laser head, power supply, vacuum pump, gas supply and long articulated arm which directs the beam to its target.

      • View 02 - View inside laser head showing CO2 laser tube and attached electrical and plumbing connections. The HeNe laser used for aiming is missing.

    • Laser Industries Sharplan Model 1100 Carbon Dioxide Laser, 140 W (Gallery)

      (The following 3 photos and descriptions provided courtesy of: Tom Rodstrom (tomrod@abac.com).)

      This is a Sharplan model 1100, 140 W CO2 Laser used for medical/surgical applications. (Unit was removed from a hospital.)

      • View 01 - Overall view. This is the main laser unit containing the laser head, power supply, vacuum pump, gas supply and long articulated arm which directs the beam to its target. There is also a 2 mW HeNe aiming laser inside the head (not visible).

      • View 02 - Interior showing vacuum pump and other stuff including cooling fan and handy-dandy funnel - purpose unknown.

      • View 03 - Interior showing gas supply bottles and regulators.
    There is much more information on carbon dioxide lasers in Sam's Laser FAQ beginning with the chapter: Carbon Dioxide Lasers and Home-Built Lasers Types and Information.
