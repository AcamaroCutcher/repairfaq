    Photos of Florod Corporation YAG Laser System

    This is a Florod Corporation LCP YAG laser trimming system for semiconductor wafers with adjustment X and Y spot size (aperture) and laser energy. It is a low energy flashlamp pumped Nd:YAG doubled (532 nm) laser that includes a CCD camera mount, single shot and 1 Hz repetition rate options. A more complete description of this system can be found in the chapter: Solid State Lasers of Sam's Laser FAQ.
