Introduction

There are literally hundreds of different commercial coil designs used for TMS and other neurological applications. Here is a summary of a few commercial figure-8 coils, mostly found in research papers, as well as the author's Sam #13 (built) and Sam #14 (proposed): 

    Mfr/Model      #Turns  ID     Mean      OD    Overlap  Wire Size   Angle
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Magstim 70 mm       9x2 5.2 cm  7.0 cm   8.8 cm  0.0 cm 1x7 mm          0 deg
 Medtronic MC-B70   10x2 2.4 cm  6.6 cm  10.8 cm  4.2 cm 3.5 mm (#8)  34.5 deg
 MagVenture CoolB70 11x2 2.3 cm  5.95 cm  9.6 cm ?3.6 cm 3x12 mm?     30.0 deg
 MagVenture MC-B70  10x2 2.7 cm  6.2 cm   9.7 cm ?3.5 cm 3x6 mm?      30.0 deg
 Sam #13             8+9 3.13 cm 5.72 cm  8.26 cm 2.5 cm 2.6 mm (#10) 30.0 deg
 Sam #14            10x2 2.9 cm  6.8 cm  10.5 cm  3.4 cm 3.18 mm Tube 30.0 deg

Calculations show the overlapped configuration to have more localization and a slightly higher peak field and dB/dt for the same inductance.

Commercial TMS Coils

The following are specifications and design info obtained from various sources including the manufacturers' Web sites and research papers. Only coils where actual engineering specifications (or at least descriptions) provided are included here. Those with only marketing blurb were ignored, though some was unavoidable for those that made the cut. :) For descriptions of the authors coils, see Sam's TMS Coils.

Medtronic MC-B70 double coil

A photo and two X-ray pictures of the Magstim figure-of-eight coil are shown in the paper: Axel Thielschera and Thomas Kammerb, "Electric field properties of two commercial figure-8 coils in TMS: calculation of focality and efficiency", Clin Neurophysiol 2004 Jul;115(7):1697-708. 

Coil Winding Data

    Inner diameter 24 mm
    Outer diameter 108 mm
    Wire cross-section: 3.5 mm
    Number of windings 2 x 10
    Thickness of plastic chassis between coils and head 2 mm.

In the coil center the windings of the two wings overlap. Next to the overlap, the windings of the left and right wings are bent by an angle of 18.5 and 16 degrees, respectively.

MagVenture Cool-B70 Butterfly

Electrical and magnetic properties similar to the MCF-B70. Designed for demanding stimulation protocols requiring a very high number of stimuli. Desined with a slightly bent surface for best possible skull contact. Equipped with trigger button to support clinical operation. Built in EPV timer and counter (5 years or 18 million EPV, which ever comes first). Number of pulses remaining = 17,472,068. 

Mechanical Properties:

    Weight of transducer head: 2.9 kg.
    Cable length: 1.3 m.
    Dimensions of transducer head: 180 x 116 x 45/64 mm.
    Angulated: 150 degrees.

Coil Winding Data:

    Inner diameter: 23 mm.
    Outer diameter: 96 mm.
    Winding height: 12 mm.
    Number of windings: 2 x 11.

Magnetic and Electrical Properties:

    Max initial dB/dt: 28 kT/s near the coil surface.
    Active pulse width: 280 µs (Biphasic).

Performance:

    Number of stimulations, before warm-up at ambient temperature of 20 °C
     wotj output of 75% of maximum at 2 pps: >20.000 pulses.

    Number of stimulations, before warm-up at ambient temperature of 20 °C
     with protocol: 60 trains at 50 pulses/train at 10 pps with inter-train
     interval of 25 s and output = 75%: >10.000 pulses.
