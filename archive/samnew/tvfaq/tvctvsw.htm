Color television standards worldwide

(The following is from EDMUNDO, Design Engineer Ten-Lab. This and additional information are available at: http://www.tenlab.com/format.htm).

We at Ten-Lab have put together the following chart listing countries and their corresponding color TV standards.

We are trying to be as accurate as possible, but we need your feedback to refine and correct the information. We are doing the best we can in spite of inherent problems such as:

  1. Some of the literature and charts are contradictory; even some books and manuals contradict each other more than they agree.

  2. Many countries have changed their names during the last few years.

  3. Some countries have one broadcast TV system, but also receive programs in a different system from beyond their borders. This creates some confusion about the format(s) used locally.
