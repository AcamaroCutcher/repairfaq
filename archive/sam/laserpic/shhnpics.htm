    Photos of Spindler and Hoyer Helium-Neon Lasers

    Spindler and Hoyer HeNe lasers include the ML-500 tunable HeNe as well as unstabilized HeNe laser systems. The following is the only known Spindler and Hoyer stabilized HeNe laser system.

    • Spindler and Hoyer ZL-150 Axial Zeeman Helium-Neon Laser (Gallery)

      The Spindler and Hoyer ZL-150 is an axial Zeeman two-frequency HeNe laser like those from HP/Agilent, but the output is right and left circularly polarized rather than horizontally and vertically linearly polarized and the split frequency is lower. It uses a standard commercial HeNe laser tube and a different locking technique but should have similar stability. Much more information on these lasers can be found in the chapter: Commercial Stabilized HeNe Lasers of Sam's Laser FAQ.
